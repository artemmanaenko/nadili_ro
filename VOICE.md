# Site voice

- Write in natural Ukrainian, including when the source is in another language. Keep Romanian names and interface labels as the reader sees them. Explain unfamiliar terms briefly in Ukrainian on first use: `ANAF (податкова служба)`.
- Write like a colleague giving directions. Use ordinary words, precise terms, and short sentences. Skip introductions, legalese, promotional copy, stock phrases, and closing summaries.
- Start each step with a verb. In the detailed procedure, say where to go, what to click, enter or bring, and what to receive or save. Include only the details that let a newcomer complete the step. Use bullets for short lists of inputs or documents.
- Put decisions where the reader makes them. Use a short aside beside the affected action for a condition, exception, or risk. Give a blocking prerequisite before the action it blocks.
- Keep each detail where it is used. Repeat only what the overview, checklist, or print memo needs to work on its own. Keep details that change the reader's action, choice or outcome, or prevent a material mistake. Cite supporting sources and keep legal analysis and unresolved research questions in working notes.

Example of a detailed procedure:

1. **Подайте заявку.** Відкрийте [сторінку реєстрації SRL](https://www.aprobat.ro/infiintare-srl). Натисніть **Intră în aplicație**, заповніть заявку й оплатіть її.
2. **Підготуйтеся до дзвінка.** Запишіть варіанти назви й вирішіть, чи реєструвати SRL платником ПДВ.

Опишіть консультанту, які послуги надаватимете. Він підкаже додаткові коди.
